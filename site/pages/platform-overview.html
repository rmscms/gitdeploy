Platform

One Desktop Hub For Git, Deploy, Backup, and Operations

GitDeploy Pro is designed as an operations cockpit: everything from daily Git pushes to emergency direct uploads, database checks, scheduled backups, and terminal operations can be handled without switching apps.

Mission

Replace fragmented workflows with one reliable Windows app that gives you visibility, traceability, and fast controls. Every important action can be reviewed, logged, retried, and verified.
